ABOUT ANGAT

A Premium Family Restaurant In The Heart Of Bavla

Welcome to Angat Restaurant, one of Bavla's most loved vegetarian dining destinations. Conveniently located on the Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway, Angat has become the preferred choice for families, travellers and food lovers seeking exceptional quality, authentic flavours and warm hospitality.

Our philosophy is simple— serve fresh food prepared with premium ingredients while treating every guest like family. From rich Punjabi delicacies and sizzling Chinese favourites to authentic South Indian specialties and delicious fast food, every dish reflects our passion for excellence.

Whether you're stopping during your journey, celebrating with loved ones or enjoying a relaxed family dinner, Angat offers a luxurious ambience, hygienic kitchen, attentive service and memorable dining experience that keeps guests coming back.
